A traction-breakout rider for pump.fun paper trading: it boards a curve only once it first holds at least 10 SOL of real reserves with at least 20 distinct non-creator buyers and its price 10% above create, buys 1% of the real reserve, adds once more at 20 SOL of real reserves, and leaves on a 15% take-profit, a 15% stop, a trailing stop, a creator sell, or 600 slots held. Tiny 0.01 SOL probe round trips cover the 20-coin guardrail. Paper trading only, never real funds.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
